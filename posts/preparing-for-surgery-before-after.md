Preparing for surgery can be **stressful**, but understanding what to expect helps you feel more in control. *This article describes key steps to take before surgery and after surgery to support a safe recovery.*

## Table of contents
- [Before surgery](#before-surgery)
- [After surgery](#after-surgery)
- [FAQs](#faqs)

## Before surgery

1. Talk to your doctor about your medications and any supplements you take.
2. Follow fasting instructions before anaesthesia.
3. Arrange for someone to drive you home after surgery.
4. Prepare your home for recovery (stock up on groceries, arrange a comfortable resting area).
5. Ask questions about the procedure, risks, and recovery timeline.

## After surgery

1. Follow your doctor's post-operative instructions carefully.
2. Take prescribed medications as directed and manage pain appropriately.
3. Keep the incision site clean and dry.
4. Attend all follow-up appointments.
5. Reach out to your doctor if you experience severe pain, fever, or signs of infection.

## FAQs

### How long does recovery take after surgery?

Recovery time varies depending on the type of surgery, your overall health, and adherence to post-operative care. Your surgeon will provide an estimated timeline.

### Can I exercise after surgery?

Light activity such as walking is encouraged, but avoid strenuous exercise until your doctor gives clearance. Listen to your body and progress gradually.
