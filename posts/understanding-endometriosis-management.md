Endometriosis occurs when tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows **outside the uterus**, often causing pain and infertility. *This article outlines what endometriosis is, its symptoms and how it can be managed.*

## What is endometriosis?

Endometriosis involves the growth of endometrial-like tissue outside the uterus, commonly on the ovaries, fallopian tubes and pelvic cavity. This tissue responds to hormonal changes, leading to inflammation and scar tissue.

## Symptoms of endometriosis

Symptoms include chronic pelvic pain, painful periods (dysmenorrhea), pain during intercourse, painful bowel movements or urination during menstruation and infertility. Symptoms vary between individuals.

## Management and treatment

Management options may include pain medication, hormonal therapy, and surgery (laparoscopy) to remove lesions. Lifestyle changes like regular exercise, a balanced diet and stress management may help reduce symptoms. Early diagnosis and individualized treatment are crucial.

## FAQs

### Does pregnancy cure endometriosis?

Pregnancy may temporarily relieve symptoms, but it is not a cure. Symptoms may return after childbirth and breastfeeding.

### Can endometriosis be prevented?

There is no known way to prevent endometriosis. Early diagnosis and treatment can help manage symptoms and improve quality of life.
