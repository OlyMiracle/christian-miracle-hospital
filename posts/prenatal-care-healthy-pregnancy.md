Prenatal care is **the care you receive during pregnancy** to ensure both mother and baby remain healthy. *This guide outlines essential steps for women in Nigeria and beyond.*

## Table of contents
- [Before pregnancy](#before-pregnancy)
- [During pregnancy](#during-pregnancy)

## Before pregnancy

Before conceiving, schedule a preconception visit with your healthcare provider, take folic acid, maintain a healthy weight, exercise regularly, and manage chronic conditions such as diabetes or hypertension.

## During pregnancy

Attend all scheduled prenatal appointments, eat a balanced diet rich in vitamins and minerals, stay hydrated, get moderate exercise approved by your doctor, avoid alcohol and smoking, and monitor fetal movements. Rest when needed and follow your healthcare provider's advice.

## FAQs

### How often should I see my doctor during pregnancy?

Typically, prenatal visits occur once a month until week 28, every two weeks until week 36, then weekly until delivery. Your doctor may adjust this schedule.

### Can I exercise during pregnancy?

Yes, gentle activities like walking, swimming, and prenatal yoga are generally safe, but consult your doctor before starting any exercise program.

## Disclaimer

This article provides general guidelines for prenatal care. It does not replace personalised medical advice. Always consult your obstetrician or midwife for recommendations specific to your pregnancy.
