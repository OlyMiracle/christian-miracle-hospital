Ovarian cysts are **fluid-filled sacs** that develop on or inside the ovary. Some cysts cause no symptoms, while others lead to discomfort. *This article explores the causes, symptoms and treatments of ovarian cysts to help you make informed decisions.*

## Causes of ovarian cysts

Most ovarian cysts develop as a normal part of the menstrual cycle, known as functional cysts. Other types include dermoid cysts, cystadenomas and endometriomas. Hormonal imbalances, pregnancy and severe pelvic infections can contribute to cyst formation.

## Symptoms of ovarian cysts

Common symptoms include pelvic pain, bloating, pain during intercourse, irregular periods and frequent urination. Many small cysts cause no symptoms and are found during routine pelvic exams.

## Treatment options

Treatment depends on the size and type of cyst. Options range from watchful waiting and medication (like hormonal birth control) to surgery. Your doctor may order ultrasound or blood tests to monitor the cyst before deciding on treatment.

## FAQs

### Do ovarian cysts affect fertility?

Most ovarian cysts are harmless and do not affect fertility. However, certain types (e.g., endometriomas) may impact fertility and require medical management.

### When should I see a doctor?

See a doctor if you experience severe pain, fever, vomiting, dizziness, rapid breathing, or if you suspect the cyst has ruptured or twisted (torsion).
