Fibroids are **benign tumours** that develop in the uterus. They are common among women of reproductive age. *This article explains what causes fibroids and the symptoms you might experience.*

## Causes of fibroids

Doctors do not know the exact cause of fibroids, but research suggests that hormones like oestrogen and progesterone play a role. Genetics, lifestyle factors and body weight may also influence the risk of developing fibroids.

## Symptoms of fibroids

Many women with fibroids experience no symptoms. When symptoms do occur, they can include heavy menstrual bleeding, pelvic pain, abdominal swelling and frequent urination. If you notice any of these signs, talk to your doctor for proper evaluation.

## FAQs

### Are fibroids cancerous?

Fibroids are almost always benign (non-cancerous). Very rarely, a malignant tumour can develop, but this is uncommon.

### Can fibroids affect fertility?

Some large fibroids may interfere with fertility or pregnancy. Your doctor can assess if your fibroids are affecting your reproductive health.
